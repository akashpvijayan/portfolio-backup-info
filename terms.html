Purpose and availability

Portfolio Assistant Backups is a utility operated by Akash P Vijayan for backing up and recovering his own application records. It is not offered as a public or commercial backup service. This website provides information about the utility and its Google Drive permissions.

Authorized access

The utility may access only data the account owner is entitled to use and has authorized through Google. Google Drive access and storage remain subject to Google's applicable terms and limits. The owner can revoke the utility's authorization at any time.

Recovery responsibilities

The owner is responsible for protecting Google credentials and encryption recovery material, maintaining suitable storage, and checking backup and restore results. A completed upload alone does not demonstrate that a backup can be restored. Backup or recovery operations can fail if storage, credentials, network access or recovery material are unavailable.

Privacy

The privacy policy explains the utility's data access, storage, retention and deletion practices.

Changes and contact

The operator may change or discontinue this personal utility. Any changes to these terms will be recorded on this page. Questions can be directed to the user support email displayed on the Google permission screen for Portfolio Assistant Backups.
