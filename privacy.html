Portfolio Assistant Backups · Policy prepared 11 September 2026

Akash P Vijayan operates Portfolio Assistant Backups as a personal backup utility for his own Google account. This policy covers the backup utility and this information website. Access to Google Drive begins only after the account owner completes Google's authorization process.

Data accessed and its purpose

The utility uses Google's drive.file scope to create and work with its own files and folders, and any files explicitly authorized for this app. It uses this access to upload encrypted backups, retrieve those copies for verification or recovery, and identify backups by their metadata.

The information processed includes:

  • Encrypted backup archives containing application records and a journal checkpoint.
  • Backup metadata, such as filenames, file and folder identifiers, byte counts, timestamps and integrity hashes.
  • Google OAuth credentials and tokens needed to perform authorized Drive operations.
  • Operational records of backup transfers, integrity checks and recovery checks.

It does not request unrestricted Drive access, Gmail access or access to contacts. A file's name and other metadata can remain visible to Google even when its contents are encrypted.

Storage and protection

Backup contents are encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive. The owner controls the destination account and the recovery credentials. Temporary backup inputs and restored copies may exist on the owner's computer or server while a backup or recovery check is performed.

OAuth credentials and tokens are kept in private configuration on devices used by the owner. They are not placed in this website or its public source. Google receives the authorization requests, encrypted files and metadata necessary to provide Drive storage.

Sharing and use restrictions

The utility uses Google data only to provide the backup and recovery functions described here. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

Google Drive provides cloud storage, and the owner's hosting provider operates the server used by the backup process. These providers handle data necessary to provide their services under their respective policies. The utility does not intentionally publish backup contents or grant other people access to the owner's Drive files.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The owner retains backups and recovery records until they are deliberately deleted. No automatic deletion period is promised. Deleting a Drive copy does not delete separate local backups or restored copies; these must be managed separately.

The owner can remove the app's access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized access by the utility but does not itself erase files already stored. The owner can separately delete Drive backups, empty Drive trash where appropriate, and remove local copies and saved credentials.

This information website

The website has no login, upload form, advertising or analytics scripts. It does not collect backup passwords or Google credentials. GitHub Pages, the intended public host, logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes as described in GitHub's documentation and privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For app or privacy questions, contact the operator using the user support email displayed on the Google permission screen for Portfolio Assistant Backups. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated policy date.
